How Harvest works. Five steps from sensor to staff.

  • SenseESP32, DHT22, MQ-135

    A sensor with the produce measures temperature, humidity and gas.

  • SendAbout every five minutes

    Each reading goes to Harvest.

  • AssessPublished rules

    Fixed rules set a risk indicator per batch.

  • PrioritiseInspection order

    Batches are ranked for inspection.

  • AlertObservations kept

    Staff inspect and record. Nothing is overwritten.

What Harvest will not do

  • Food safety

    Harvest is decision support, not a food-safety determination.

  • Certification

    It never certifies produce as safe.

  • Shelf life

    It never estimates shelf life.

  • Sell or discard

    It never tells anyone to sell or discard.

  • Gas readings

    The gas sensor is uncalibrated. A gas rise can be ordinary ripening.

  • Accuracy

    Piloted with a box of tomatoes. Predictive accuracy has not been validated.
